      Good to know

      Questions people ask

      How do I order a gift box?

      Use the builder on this page, or message us on WhatsApp. Either way we confirm the items and details with you before we start.

      How is a box personalized?

      Words you choose go on pieces such as the tumbler and the perfume label, and everything is wrapped by hand. We tell you which pieces can carry your words when we plan the box.

      Can I choose what goes inside?

      Yes. Start from a package or pick your own pieces. If you are unsure, choose Surprise me and tell us about the person.

      Do you deliver outside Kenya?

      We deliver worldwide. Tell us the city and country and the date you need it, and we confirm on WhatsApp.

      How long does a box take?

      It depends on the pieces and the date you need. Tell us your date and we confirm what is possible before you commit.

      Can I change my order?

      Message us as soon as you can. Changes are easiest before we start personalizing and packing.
